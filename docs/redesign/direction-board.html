The receipt is the product

The same slip appears in the flow, on the success screen, on the tracking page and in the PDF. It grows as the citizen fills it in, then gets stamped.

Citizen's words in Literata

The description is set in the reading serif on ruled lines, so it reads as testimony. Form chrome stays in Sofia Sans.

Stamp in ochre ink

The ring text carries the six stars of the flag as separators. It multiplies into the paper in light mode and turns bright gold at night.

Key on a tear-off stub

Grouped in fives so it can be read aloud or copied by hand. The stub tears free after the stamp. Leaving needs an "I saved my key" tick.

No invented data

Everything on this slip is a field the backend already stores: public ID, category, municipality, coordinates, description, photo count, submitted time, anonymous flag.

    What changed after checking the plan

    Parts of the first plan that looked like defaults, and what replaced them.

    • Vermilion stamp became ochre ink.Red would contradict the non-emergency message, and vermilion on navy is a stock look.
    • Serif display became condensed sans.A big serif on warm paper is the most common generated look and holds up worse in sunlight. The serif moved to the citizen's own words.
    • Headline-plus-card hero became the map.The map is the hero. The card is pinned to a real street, Rruga Agim Ramadani.
    • Numbered steps became the receipt assembling.The sequence is shown by the object filling in, once, without circles.
